## 16. Design history — what's been tried

**References she liked, in order:**
1. Impossible Foods (via refero) — dark canvas, oversized display type, parenthetical second line, pill CTAs.
2. ilovecreatives / Xiaohongshu collage portfolios — sky backgrounds, cut-outs, stickers, folders.
3. A crumpled-paper scrapbook template — paper clips, tilted photos, cut-paper type. **This became the current build.**
4. [akshatkhanna.framer.website](https://akshatkhanna.framer.website/) — tiled + grid background, typewriter headline, cards lifting on hover, everything settling in on scroll. Motion was adopted.
5. [chahatwahi canva site](https://clock-of-nature.my.canva.site/chahatwahi) — **the current structural model**: work first and about last, client logo bar, headline stats, one colour-blocked card per brand, outlined annotation labels on each artifact.

**What she's rejected:**
- Anything that "looks AI-made" — generic card grids, count-up stats, scroll-fade everywhere, emoji, stock gradients.
- Too much text. Copy has been cut roughly in half twice.
- Over-decorated craft styling — "childlike". Tilts and paper grain were dialled back for this reason.
- Excessive use of only indigo + yellow; the palette should be spread across sections.

**Interactive features built (worth keeping or re-imagining):**
- Handwriting draw-on that writes, erases and cycles three phrases with a pen nib.
- A folder graphic that pops the four reels open on click.
- A liquid glass blob (animated SVG turbulence) that opens the AI videos.
- Instagram-style video frames with like/comment/share icons, real like and view counts, tap-to-unmute and a link to the post.
- Videos that only load and play when scrolled into view.
