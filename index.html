Case study

Desh Ki Dukaan, in three months

One Shopify store, a college dropshipping curriculum, and a D2C lifestyle brand taken to ₹20L+ in revenue. Here's how it went.

  1. Day 0 to 4

    Launch fast, sell faster

    Built a conversion-optimised Shopify store and went live with targeted Meta Ads. ₹6L in revenue within the first four days.

  2. Day 5

    ₹1 lakh a day

    Daily sales crossed ₹1L by day five, with ad creative, offer and landing page iterated daily on live data.

  3. Weeks 2 to 4

    Fix the leak

    Return-to-origin was eating margin at 60%. Broke delivery performance down by region, cut spend on high-RTO pincodes, and brought it to 30%.

  4. Month 3

    ₹20L+ and the lowest CAC

    Crossed ₹20L in revenue with a ₹7 CAC, the lowest across every competing team. Featured in the Masters' Union Dropshipping Magazine.
