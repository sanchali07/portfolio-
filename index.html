₹20 lakh
(in three months)

Desh Ki Dukaan: one Shopify store, a college dropshipping curriculum, and a D2C lifestyle brand taken to ₹20L+ in revenue.

1

Launch fast, sell faster

Day 0 to 4. Conversion-optimised Shopify store, targeted Meta Ads. ₹6L in revenue in the first four days.

2

₹1 lakh a day

Day 5. Daily sales crossed ₹1L, with ad creative, offer and landing page iterated daily on live data.

3

Fix the leak

Weeks 2 to 4. RTO was eating margin at 60%. Broke delivery down by region, cut spend on bad pincodes, brought it to 30%.

4

Lowest CAC of every team

Month 3. ₹20L+ revenue at a ₹7 CAC. Featured in the Masters' Union Dropshipping Magazine.
