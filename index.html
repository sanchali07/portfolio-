Case study

₹20 lakh
in 3 months

Desh Ki Dukaan: one Shopify store, a college dropshipping curriculum, and a D2C lifestyle brand taken to ₹20L+ in revenue. Here's how it went.

Desh Ki Dukaan
Nov 2023 – Jan 2024

  1. Day 0 to 4 Conversion-optimised Shopify store, targeted Meta Ads. ₹6L in revenue in the first four days.
  2. Day 5 Daily sales crossed ₹1L, with ad creative, offer and landing page iterated daily on live data.
  3. Weeks 2 to 4 RTO was eating margin at 60%. Broke delivery down by region, cut spend on bad pincodes, brought it to 30%.
  4. Month 3 ₹20L+ revenue at a ₹7 CAC, the lowest of every competing team. Featured in the Masters' Union Dropshipping Magazine.
